This policy explains what information the UdyamSetu Pvt. Ltd. website ("the website") collects, why we collect it and how we use it. It applies to this website only.

## Information we collect

**Information you send us.** When you use a form on the website, such as the contact form, a collaboration enquiry, a research proposal, an expression of interest or the newsletter sign-up, you send us the information that you enter. This usually includes your name, your email address, your organisation and your message.

**Website usage information.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the website is used. It counts page views and records information such as the page visited, the referring website, the type of browser and device, and the country. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not follow you across other websites.

**Technical information.** The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver the website and protect it from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical information such as IP addresses.

## How we use information

- To reply to your enquiry, proposal or application.
- To send you updates, if you signed up for the newsletter. You can ask us to remove you from the list at any time.
- To understand which pages are useful and to improve the website.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

## Forms and other services

Our forms are provided by Tally (tally.so). The information that you enter in a form goes to Tally, which stores it for us. Tally's own privacy policy applies to how Tally processes this information.

Some pages can show content from other services, such as videos from YouTube or a map from OpenStreetMap. These services can collect information when they load or when you use them, under their own privacy policies. Videos load only when you choose to play them.

## Cookies

The website does not set its own cookies. Services such as Tally forms and embedded videos can set their own cookies when you use them.

## How long we keep information

We keep enquiries, proposals and applications for as long as we need them to reply to you and to keep proper records. After that, we delete them.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information that you have sent us. Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, can give you further rights. To make a request, use the details on our [Contact page](/contact/).

## Children

The website is not directed at children. Do not send us personal information about a child without the consent of a parent or guardian.

## Changes to this policy

We can update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, use the details on our [Contact page](/contact/).
